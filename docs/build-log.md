## 4 Nov 2026 - rudder depth x taper sweep (rudder_sweep.py, analysis only)

New decision-record script rudder_sweep.py for the question of a longer rudder blade with a stronger taper. No spec, opt.py or documented number was changed; every figure below is PROPOSED and is for Lukas to confirm.

Baseline reproduced: 220 mm, 34 mm, 17%, 4 plies gives a 1.21 deg limit at 22.15 kt (rudder_limit.py: 1.2), 6.29 Nm and a 5.8 mm stock at the hull bottom (6.3 Nm, 5.8 mm), blade drag 0.92 N at 10 kt (0.93) and rake moment 1.53 Nm (1.55).

Findings (details in the script output; strip drag is a delta against the baseline because the absolute strut drag is calibrated):
- The model has no term that rewards depth, so depth was treated as a constraint. At 220 mm the T-foil is level with the main foil at the design ride (zero margin). With heel to leeward the centreline rudder rises 364.6 mm x sin(heel) relative to the foil: 36 mm at 5.7 deg (the main foil's own ventilation limit, specs/03). Staying level with the foil needs 257 mm at 5.7 deg and 0 deg pitch, 265 mm with 1 deg of bow-down pitch, 302 mm at 10 deg / 2 deg. The envelope is an assumption.
- Staying 3 T-foil chords (90 mm) under water instead needs 219 mm at 5.7 deg / 1 deg, 228 mm at 5.7 / 2 deg and 256 mm at 10 / 2 deg: the current 220 mm meets it at 5.7 deg of heel with up to 1 deg of bow-down pitch (91 mm); at 2 deg it needs 228 mm. This holds the main foil at its design depth; the decided ride-height feedforward raises the hull as heel increases, which would make the T-foil shallower by the same amount (assumption, not modelled).
- Taper helps, more than expected: 220 mm with 34 -> 20 mm raises the limit to 1.60 deg, cuts blade drag 0.11 N at 10 kt and 3 g of mass, and moves upwind +0.2 to +0.3 kt in the VPP (the VPP speed step is about 0.1 kt, so differences under 0.1 kt are not resolved). In this model taper never stops paying: the gain keeps growing down to a 14 mm tip. The floor is set by the T-foil junction (30 mm chord on a 14-24 mm tip) and by how far NeuralFoil's drag below 100k Reynolds number is trusted (a 20 mm tip at 6.5 kt is about 67k; the drag table clamps at 50k): both are Lukas's decisions. Taper does not reduce the force at the limit (about 31-34 N), so steering authority is kept.
- Depth costs speed: about 0.5 kt upwind per +40 mm with the same taper. 240 mm with 34 -> 20 mm is level with the baseline blade on speed (within one VPP step, about 0.1 kt) and on strength (1.22 deg, 4 plies, 11.8 g); 260 mm with 40 -> 22 mm costs 0.3 kt upwind, 1.27 deg with 4 plies, 15.5 g, and a 6.4 mm stock.
- Not modelled or unverified: blade ventilation, the T-foil junction on a 20-24 mm tip (the T-foil chord is 30 mm), NeuralFoil accuracy below 100k Reynolds number, load distribution along the blade (uniform per chord, conservative). Research on the real AC40 / AC75 rudder depth failed: the session's network policy blocked the pages, so no full-scale figure is used (hosts refused: www.zeilen.nl, www.americascup.com, en.wikipedia.org, www.yachtingworld.com, www.sailsmagazine.com.au; the Drive docs only quote the AC75 cant-axis figures). The shortlist is not 3D-validated (CLAUDE.md rule 2): no saved foil.tools wing analyses exist and analyze_wing credits were not spent.

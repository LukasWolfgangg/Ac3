# Design & Specs - 1. Principal Dimensions and 2. Hull

Source: Google Doc "Design & Specs" (30 Oct 2026). Sources of truth for numbers; status tags as in 00-concept-and-decision-log.md.

## 1. Principal Dimensions

LOA 1000 mm (fixed); beam 286 mm and depth 119 mm on Hull_v2 (PROPOSED, section 2). Reach 250 mm/side (DECIDED cap), drop 270 mm; both are built into the redrawn arm, Foilarm_v3 (27 Sep 2026). All-up weight: see the Weight Budget spreadsheet (the only place it is stated; nominal 2800 g with the ballast trimmed, target 2800 g).

Arm shape (curved, fully-down position). CAD (Foilarm_v3, 27 Sep 2026): a straight vertical run of 135 mm from the foil line, then a bend of roughly 50-60 mm radius and a run rising toward the pivot end (about 18 deg above horizontal near the bend, steepening toward the pivot); total path length foil-to-pivot 432 mm. The steel-to-carbon joint sits at the top of the straight run (section 3). The analysis scripts still use the path traced from a screenshot of Foilarm_v2 (20 Sep 2026, good to about +-10 mm) and stretched horizontally to 250 mm reach: about 426 mm long with a straight run of about 139 mm, archived as arm_pts.csv in the Simulation & Analysis Drive folder (a 100-point resample that reproduces the full 716-point trace's path integrals to <0.03%). The differences from the CAD path are inside the tracing tolerance.

## 2. Hull

- **Hull v2, AC40 shape (rev 4): PROPOSED, 27 Oct 2026.** Replaces Hull_v1, which was lofted from the AC75 at about 1:20.7 and came out undersized. Hull_v2 is the AC40 (11.8 m LOA, 3.38 m beam) at 1:11.8: LOA 1000 mm, beam 286 mm at the knuckle, depth 119 mm. The profile is traced from McConaghy's AC40 side elevation. The deck stays high to a near-vertical stem with a tight top corner and a rounded forefoot; the knuckle (the maximum-beam crease) runs low amidships and rises to just under the deck at the stem; the bottom is a full bilge amidships and a hollow V forward, with a centreline bustle up to 29.5 mm deep; the plan is pointed. CAD: Fusion document Hull_v2, version 3. Lines, renders and the bow comparison are on the Hull v2 page (claude.ai artifact X19NtKr1X5gCb8Yd8jnbzR).
- **Hydrostatics at 2.80 kg (Hull_v2, PROPOSED):** draft 47.4 mm; waterline 980 x 270 mm; LCB 443 mm from the transom (44.3% of LOA); prismatic coefficient 0.646; waterplane 1876 cm2; wetted surface 2095 cm2; 0.53 mm of sinkage per 100 g. The knuckle sits 9.6 mm above the waterline amidships. Level trim at rest is not a requirement: with the current layout (section 8) the all-up CG sits 39 mm forward of the LCB, which trims the boat about 0.44 deg bow down at rest (about 4 mm at the ends); putting the CG on the LCB would cut the pitch-stability margin while foiling to about 19 mm.
- **Arm pivot on Hull_v2 (84%, DECIDED 28 Oct 2026):** at the pivot station, 531 mm from the transom, the half-beam is 136.5 mm. At 86% (117.4 mm off the centreline) the outboard fork cheek of the rev D gimbal cuts 0.7 mm through the skin at 40 deg of cant; at 84% (114.6 mm, the AC75's figure) it clears by 2.0 mm, with the cant axis 30.6 mm above the waterline. The 2.0 mm is measured to the outside of the hull, so about 1.8 mm remains inside one ply; on the gimbal rev E (28 Oct 2026) the fork is trimmed to 17.5 mm from the cant axis and only the carbon arm passes through the skin (Mechanisms - Foil Arm section 2d); fair over the arm openings. At 114.6 mm the righting-moment lever is 364.6 mm; on the structural basis (section 3) the righting moment is 15.12 Nm and the moment carried across the hull 4.75 Nm; the width with both foils down is about 1029 mm. opt.py now uses PIVOT_Y 0.1146 m and the Hull_v2 waterline length, 0.98 m (re-run 28 Oct 2026, section 11).
- **Skin area on Hull_v2:** 0.58 m2 of hull and deck, about 253 g in one ply of 200 g/m2 carbon (taken as 440 g/m2 cured with resin), now in the Weight Budget (28 Oct 2026: hull shell 122.3 g, deck and upper topsides 131.0 g, by face area); the 51 g over the old 202 g came out of the ballast (section 9).
- **Skin/layup:** CONFIRMED - solid, no-core, 1-ply 200 g/m2 carbon fiber, non-structural hull and deck skin.
- **Internal structure:** carries about 0.13 kg of lead ballast between the pivot bulkheads; the root mounts react the 13.48 Nm design cant torque plus the 4.75 Nm (static) carried across the hull from the pivot offset (section 3). In the Weight Budget the pivot bulkheads and well walls (88 g) come from the gimbal rev E CAD; the rest (77 g) is a rough scaling, not a structural design (section 10).
